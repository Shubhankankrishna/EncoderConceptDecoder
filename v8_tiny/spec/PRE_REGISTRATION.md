# V8 12-hour engineering pilot

## Scope and hypothesis

This pilot tests whether correcting V7's exposure deficit yields a competent V6-style encoder/concept/decoder model, whether delayed next-concept loss helps against alpha-zero and an ordinary Transformer, and—conditionally on competence—whether replacement, exploratory control, search and local-support continual learning preserve useful behavior. It is not a complete reproduction of the architecture papers, independent-seed confirmation, empirical scaling study or LLM-quality test.

The source snapshot is `draft_v8.md`. The executable code is self-contained. `LOCK.json` fixes its hash, corpus manifest, optimizer/exposure protocol and stage priorities before detached execution.

## Model and foundation protocol

E3/C4/D3, d128, four heads, gated FF384, vocabulary2048, four-token completed document-local pooling, eight PQ segments with32 codes, continuous residual port and gamma0.1. No added QK normalization, initialization change, IRC/CRC or hidden fully discrete interpretation. Bias-free smooth rank16 Z injection is zero anchored. The ordinary baseline uses10 ordinary causal pre-RMS/RoPE blocks, FF400 on nine blocks and384 on the tenth; tied embedding. Learned counts are2,448,640 and2,450,048. Full recurrence and full frozen-decoder autograd are retained in replacement/exploration training.

Primary seed811; identical foundation sampler seed81811 across arms. Foundation arms are alpha-zero, delayed NCP alpha0 through update6000, ramp to0.1 during6000–8000, then0.1; and the ordinary Transformer. Fresh initialization for every primary arm. Engineering weights never initialize a foundation. Each arm gets24,000 updates at effective batch64 and192 positions, with32 text,24 GraphNav,4 lookup,2 composition and2 pointer rows. This is294,912,000 input slots and576,000 GraphNav examples per completed arm; nonpadding/scored targets and unique identities are logged separately. Text sources are selected by cumulative scored targets to maintain equal TinyStories/FineWeb-Edu exposure. Symbolic labels initially retain V6's hidden random-walk color-segment/duration distribution; legal alternative paths receive success. Main graphs have10 nodes; the enlarged hard continuation is an isolated stage.

AdamW betas0.9/0.95, epsilon1e-8, peak0.002, matrix weight decay0.1, no bias/norm decay, clip1, warmup300 and cosine floor10%. PQ codebooks use10× learning rate and no decay; dead-row restart every250 updates before80% of the original horizon, zeroing affected moments. VQ uses segment squared-distance sums (not a16× smaller feature mean). Next-concept targets are detached pooled features in the next valid same-document chunk; scored target eligibility follows the exact next-token shift.

## Engineering and promotion gates

Before primary training, cap fixed128 lookup/composition memorization at2,000 updates (require99% strict free-generation success), then fresh lookup/two-hop learning separately for hierarchy and flat models at6,000 updates (512 development items; require95% and90%). Screens run every500 updates, stopping early only on passing. If these fail, do not train an expensive primary matrix and do not claim architecture failure; archive the learning curve and report the shared recipe problem.

Primary standard/lookup development suites are fixed at2,048 items. The competence thresholds are lookup≥95% with95% Wilson lower≥90%, first complete legal graph move≥98%, and standard strict success≥80% with lower≥75%. Both raw scores and failure modes are retained for all arms. Alpha-zero may support a component study if NCP fails; that does not confirm the NCP hypothesis. Prefer delayed NCP if competent, otherwise alpha-zero. Operational selection uses development only; sealed testing never reselects an anchor.

Preservation requires absolute competence plus paired symbolic retention lower≥−2 percentage points and text NLL increase upper≤0.02. Paired intervals use Bonferroni exact binomial bounds on gain/loss probabilities, including zero discordance. Text intervals resample document clusters; repeated windows are not independent documents. Symbolic retention checks adjust interval alpha across four retained endpoints. Text intervals are pilot, pointwise bootstrap intervals: no familywise confirmatory claim is made. Tiny sample/gate uncertainty is reported as unresolved; no adaptive sample increase to force acceptance.

## Conditional stages and budget priority

1. Complete all three foundations at full exposure before widening controls. Partial foundations cannot be anchors. A stage never silently reduces24,000 updates to fit the clock.
2. If an anchor is competent and time permits,8,000-update384-position hard continuation (10/14/18-node GraphNav mix) with retained optimizer moments, peak0.0005, warmup200 and reset LR horizon. Accept only with standard/lookup/text preservation. Primary hard-gain inference remains exploratory in this pilot.
3. Dense exact-copy and six-visit recurrent replacement,6,000 updates each. Frozen E/D/PQ/gamma; peak0.001; warmup200. Teacher U/A/soft-PQ MSE starts at1, decays after10% and ends by30%. A matched dense refit without teacher penalty is budgeted next. Zero-copy identity is checked independently. Unresolved retention rejects promotion. The dense accepted refit has priority over recurrent to avoid post-test architecture selection. Historical unused teacher weights remain resident for auditing; active and resident parameters are reported separately. This experiment is not a deployment export/folding test.
4. Two-site smooth Z adapters,6,000 updates; two independently sampled controls, held1/2/4 chunks, beta0.25 best-candidate/mean row loss. Freeze deterministic anchor and train only Z. No zero-control competitor and no random-Z NCP. Codebook and zero-anchor preservation must pass. Coverage uses512 hard10 items with eight prior draws: zero greedy≥10%, prior-only≥20% with lower>10%,≥64 baseline failures,≥5 distinct rescues, rescue≥5% with lower>1%. Failure blocks expensive search, rather than spending hours on zeros.
5. Three-phase LSL and separate sequential always-open LoRA control. Fit negative support once at accepted P0/Z0 on1M visible input slots; it includes old-distribution graph answers and has disclosed reference-data access. Per-block fixed projection d128→32, diagonal negative8/positive4 GMM, token-uniform priority reservoir≤65,536, max50 EM iterations, tolerance1e-4, three random-reservoir initializations (NumPy/SciPy EM, avoiding mixed OpenMP runtimes), floor max(1e-6,1e-4 reference variance). Log valid counts and symbolic header/answer proportions. Store32 extra reference-variance scalars per group for the positive-fit variance floor; buffer counts include this addition. New rank4 Q/V banks are random-A/zero-B, scale2, trained forced open; previous gates stay active. Primary rho1, hard log-density difference>0. Older weights/densities stay frozen and differentiable with respect to new upstream inputs.2,000 updates/B64 per phase, LR0.0003/warmup100/no decay. Fit positive support only after forced-open learning establishes gain. Evaluate the SAME weights with new gate forced open to isolate installation loss. New text gain must have an upper delta-NLL bound<0; symbolic gain≥5pp with positive lower bound. Installation margin2pp/0.02nats, original-task adjacent and cumulative retention, and retention of earlier accepted new tasks decide promotion. A failed LSL candidate is archived and later promoted phases stop. The separate always-open trajectory may continue three phases as a preregistered research-only forgetting control. Flat LSL gets the same bank/gate budget if its backbone is competent and a whole trajectory fits. No concept-only, reverse-order or replay comparison is silently inferred.
6. GradCuit and AB-MCTS are conditional on actual exploration coverage. Freeze weights and regenerate every proposal. Use rejected decision-slot mean log-probability plus same-version zero KL; masked Adam0.9/0.99 with per-coordinate clocks. Tree edges use fresh state; sequential repair retains moments. GEN/CONT Beta counts are tested. Graded public-header feedback uses the draft formula and never a hidden BFS/reference gradient. Tuned retry, latent resampling, combined sampling, GradCuit, nonadaptive same-kernel allocation, AB-prior, AB-hybrid and AB-hybrid equal-norm random-direction control are compared under one common synchronized budget derived from flat zero+15 retries, calibration early-stop disabled across attempts. Candidate cap64/depth16/duplicate-attempt cap8. Six retry and six gradient settings get equal eight-development-problem tuning sets. Search test blocks interleave method order and stop before starting a block that will not fit. Success earns credit only after verification before deadline. Unfinished work is charged. Trees store histories and at most one live decoding cache; caches are rebuilt per candidate, so no shared mutable prefix cache can leak between branches. This costs more prefill but is a valid charged-cost comparison. Search RNG is not a training seed.

The queue reserves90 minutes of the12-hour ceiling for sealed evaluation and reporting. It may finish earlier after a learning/competence gate failure. Its calibrated stage estimates protect complete exposure; insufficient budget skips later stages. It does not weaken a gate or promote a partial checkpoint. Every skip and partial exposure appears in the final report.

## Dataset and controls

Foundation TinyStories and FineWeb-Edu documents have new document-disjoint V8 partitions. Existing corpus provenance and prior lexical near-deduplication are retained, but this is not newly sourced external data. Historical checkpoints may have seen these corpora. Canonical symbolic queries are excluded across all generated dev/test suites and from every training/gate-fit sampler. Actual seen hashes are tracked and checkpointed.

Continual text withholds hosts containing Wikipedia, Britannica, ScienceDaily, Phys.org, Scientific American, New Scientist, NPS or MathForum:561 training/69 development/69 test documents. Its modest independent document count and repeated training windows are explicit. Phase2 uses an8-token `[EOS,start,c1,c2,GNAV,c3,c4,SEP]` query, followed by the ordinary graph, asking for the terminal node after four colored transitions. Phase3 uses `[c2,goal,start,SEP]` plus `[destination,source,color,TO]` edges and ordered node labels. It requests a legal goal-reaching walk. The marker makes the semantics observable; there are no identical prompts with contradictory answers. Prompt-marker routing alone can explain some gating; do not claim semantic support separation without per-layer behavior evidence.

The V6 bridge replays the existing9,000-update alpha-zero and NCP checkpoints on inspected V6 development examples. It does NOT count as retraining, an independent seed, or a fresh common-protocol quality comparison. Trained decoder checks include real graph prefixes at every modulo-four offset, causal/document isolation, six recurrent visit caches, Z-zero/LoRA-zero behavior, positive gradients through frozen networks and branch smoothing state. MPS FP32 cache tolerance is4e-4 absolute plus small relative tolerance; mathematically zero B/control writes are checked separately. Compilation requires eager-loss/parameter-gradient parity per path; a failed parity path runs eager and the deviation is logged.

## Explicitly deferred or incomplete draft claims

These are marked untested, not assumed successful. Any result here is a conditional single-seed pilot effect. Execution scores and passed smoke tests cannot establish model quality.
