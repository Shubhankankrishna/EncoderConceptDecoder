The queue, and how results become decisions

What has run, what is running, what is waiting, and the rules that turn a number into a decision.

The queue

All experiments share one laptop GPU, so they run one after another. The order was chosen by the project owner: finish S1, then the dense half of S2, then S6's first experiment, then the recurrent half of S2, then S3, S4, S5 and the final analysis. S2 and S6 do not run at the same time, because sharing the GPU would save no time and would spoil the speed measurements.

How a result becomes a decision

A number from a run is never the decision. Three habits stand between a number and a conclusion.

  1. Zones, not points.

    Every gate has a pass zone, a partial band and a fail zone, and the whole 95% interval must sit in a zone for it to count. The slider figure below shows how it works.

  2. Paired comparisons.

    Two arms are compared on the same puzzles, one by one. For each puzzle we ask whether arm A solved it and B did not, or the reverse. In S1, on seed 902, delayed beat no-NCP on 121 puzzles and lost on 101: a gap that is too small to rule out luck.

  3. At least two seeds.

    A gate passes only if both seeds pass. One pass and one fail is "seed-dependent", and counts as inconclusive. S1 showed how much one seed can mislead.

What V9 cannot answer

The design lists its own limits. In plain words:

  • Searching over steps in ordinary text. Text has no clean step boundaries, so this is future work.
  • Anything above the small model (2.45 million parameters), unless the optional larger sizes are run. The big NCP study started at 3 billion.
  • Whether a cheaper search becomes a faster search on other hardware or at other widths.
  • Whether the judges stay reliable on imagined positions deeper than four steps.
  • Whether the recurrent core behaves differently in S3 to S5, because only the dense model is promoted there.
  • Whether any of this works over many phases of continual learning, since only three are run.
  • Whether skills carry over to bigger puzzles. For every earlier model, hard14 and long18 stayed near zero.
What to watch next

The next milestone is the end of S2's dense tier: the last cell finishes training, then its measurement, then the V8 replay, and then a program applies the pre-registered gates to both seeds and writes the promotion decision. Whatever it says, S3 to S5 run, are limited, or stop exactly as the rules say.
